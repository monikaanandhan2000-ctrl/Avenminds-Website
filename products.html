Built for one team. Useful to every industry.

Every AvenMinds product started life solving a problem for one of our own service teams — the LMS was built to run our EdTech batches, ComplyTrack was built to keep our own finance desk on schedule. That's why they hold up under real, daily use rather than sitting as a feature checklist.

Because our teams work across ten service verticals, the same platform often ends up useful well beyond the domain it was built for. A hospital uses HRMS for staff onboarding the same way a manufacturing unit uses it for shift-floor hiring; a boutique uses Site Builder for its storefront the same way a coaching institute uses it for admissions.

For real estate and construction businesses, PropertyDesk, ProjectTrack and RealEstate Pro mean leads, site visits and project milestones live in one place instead of scattered spreadsheets — useful whether you're a single broker or managing multiple ongoing sites.

For finance, audit and compliance-heavy businesses, ERP Lite, ComplyTrack and FinEdge take the pressure off manual tracking — bookkeeping, GST, TDS and ROC deadlines are visible well before they become urgent, whichever industry you're filing for.

For education providers and training teams, the LMS, Exam Prep and Mentor Connect scale a single instructor's reach — live sessions, recorded content, mock assessments and mentoring all run through one system, whether it's a coaching centre, a corporate L&D team, or a hospital training its own staff.

And for sector-specific teams — healthcare, retail, media, manufacturing, hospitality, fashion, non-profits and government — HealthSuite, Commerce Cloud, MediaHub, Manufacturing Suite, Hospitality Manager, Lifestyle Studio, NGO Connect and GovSuite bring the same in-house discipline to workflows that used to run on spreadsheets and phone calls. None of these are locked to the sector they're named after — we regularly configure them across industries, and our team handles the setup either way.
